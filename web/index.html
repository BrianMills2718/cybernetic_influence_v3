      Read me
      
          How to read a cybernetic simulation
          
      
          This is a bounded causal model, not a claim that a real organization is a single mind. People and exact processes act; records, credentials, and mechanisms constrain what can happen; an organization is an analytical composite over those retained components.
          
      
          
        
          Choose and play
          Select a scenario and condition, then run it. Live runs let LLM-modeled people act from their retained memory, position, observations, and interfaces. Reference runs use fixed zero-cost policies.
          
        
          Read the map
          Spatial layout shows authored places, occupants, and physical links. Causal flow shows information, actions, records, and mechanisms. Spatial proximity does not grant authority or communication.
          
        
          Change analytical scale
          The graph’s analytical-scale control collapses or expands an execution-inert composite such as the service desk. It changes what is summarized, not what exists or acts in the simulation.
          
        
          Follow the account
          The narrative is an LLM-written account grounded in each moment’s retained trace. Use the rolled-up causal-moment inspector and advanced evidence when you need to step down to exact events and state.
