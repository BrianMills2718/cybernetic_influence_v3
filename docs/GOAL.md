# Research MVP Continuous-Execution Goal

## Goal

**Mission:** Deliver the remaining agent-executable work for a reviewable
cybernetic multiscale-agency research MVP: a user can run a naturally
multi-episode coordination scenario, understand its causal arc, inspect exact
evidence, distinguish exact from evidence-coded measures, and compare baseline,
heterogeneous-pressure, and stabilization conditions.

**Execution profile:** `continuous-light`.

**Stage and investment boundary:** Private functional PoC. Follow the thirteen
ordered packets in the canonical
[roadmap](ROADMAP.md#ordered-mvp-completion-packets). Do not expand into
production hardening, public hosting, evasion variants, or the composite-agency
assay merely because work remains executable.

**Canonical example:** An analyst selects the synthetic multinational
bio-surveillance decision, reviews one condition, runs a 12–28 meaningful-
moment live trajectory, reads concise and detailed narrative prose grounded in
simulator-owned evidence context, inspects spatial topology/configured
pathways/realized causal graph, then compares two valid trajectories per
condition with exact and evidence-coded measures visibly separated.

**Repository:** the repository root containing this document. The root workspace
instructions, canonical roadmap, [Slice 20](plans/020-inspectable-extended-runs.md),
and [Slice 21](plans/021-coordination-environment-assay.md) are binding.

## Boundaries

- Execute exactly one roadmap packet at a time in a claimed linked worktree.
- Preserve atomic people and mechanisms as executors; organizations and other
  aggregates remain reversible, execution-inert analytical views.
- Preserve the three graph meanings and exact mechanism authority. Do not add
  a generalized game master, organization executor, scalar trust/risk/readiness
  or agency score, generated mechanism code, or procedural commands in people.
- Use the existing shared `llm_client`; do not modify it or add direct provider
  calls. Every LLM call must retain `task`, `trace_id`, `max_budget`, native
  `json_schema`, and provider-observed cost.
- Do not deploy, certify a changed route, make a paid provider call, replace an
  invalid paid run, switch models, or relax a schema without the explicit
  authorization required by the current packet.
- Do not begin Slice 21 before 20D2 is complete. Do not begin 21B before the
  21A scripted/live readouts. Do not begin 21C before 21B controls and live
  canaries pass. Do not begin 21D or Slice 22 under this goal.
- Preserve unrelated work and fail loudly. No silent fallback, synthesized
  narrative, invented evidence, hidden retries, or weakened negative fixture.

## Execution Increments

Execute the ordered packet table in the roadmap. The detailed packet is the
implementation authority; this goal does not duplicate it. For each packet:

1. verify its preconditions and ownership;
2. implement only its named behavior and both-sign checks;
3. run its exact verification commands;
4. audit the complete owned diff;
5. commit and push the coherent packet;
6. update only the canonical status/progress authority when observed truth
   changed; and
7. stop at the packet's explicit authorization or human-readout boundary.

At an authorization/readout boundary, finish every safe zero-cost preparation,
report the exact revision, topology, per-call request budgets, planning amount,
evidence, question, and resume event, then return control. Absence of
authorization is not permission, goal
completion, or a technical failure.

## Loop Bounds

- Make at most three materially different attempts on the same reproduced
  blocker. If they yield no new evidence or safe next action, report the exact
  blocker, owner, retained evidence, and resume event; do not invent a fallback.
- The finite work sequence is the thirteen roadmap packets ending at 21C2.
  Completing a packet never adds another packet to this goal; scope expansion
  requires an explicit replacement goal.
- Never perform a fourth consecutive process-only increment. Revalidate the
  shortest path to the canonical observable behavior instead.
- Revalidate strategy after three completed packets, roughly four hours of
  continuous work, twice a packet's stated effort, or any roadmap/outcome
  reset. Report user-visible outcome progress separately from enabling and
  process work, then recommend `retain`, `replace`, or `clear` for this goal.
- If the goal becomes misaligned, stop substantive execution and return control
  for replacement. Do not call strategic misalignment a technical blocker.

## External Resume Events

These events control when agent work may resume; they are not evidence the
agent may fabricate or completion criteria it may waive:

- explicit certification/deployment/spend authorization for 20D2, 21A4, 21B2,
  and 21C1 after their exact topology and cap are reported;
- operator readout at the 20D2, 21A2/21A4, 21B2, and 21C2 boundaries; and
- authorization for any paid replacement of an invalid comparison run.

If one is absent, report the pending event once and stop active execution. When
the user supplies it, resume the same goal at the named packet without
restarting completed work.

## Done

The agent-executable goal is complete only when C1–C6 are demonstrated and
reported, the research-MVP comparison is technically ready for the operator's
final judgment, all owned changes are committed and pushed on canonical main,
and no required implementation or verification packet remains. Do not claim
empirical validity, prediction, real-world attribution, production readiness,
or completion of the broader North Star.

The operator's final usefulness judgment remains the operator's decision. If
it is not yet supplied, report the technically complete state and exact review
request rather than looping or manufacturing approval.
