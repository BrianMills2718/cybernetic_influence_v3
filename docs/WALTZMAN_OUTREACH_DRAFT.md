# Waltzman outreach draft

## Subject

An executable demonstration inspired by *From Minds to Coordination*

## Email

Rand—

I built a small multi-agent simulation inspired by *From Minds to
Coordination*, and I would value your reaction to whether it captures the
mechanism you had in mind.

Twenty-six autonomous AI roles decide whether to activate a cross-border
early-warning compact. Between rounds, four separate AI source agents observe
the public coalition result and introduce technical, legal, logistical, and
community signals. The sources cannot vote, recommend a vote, or modify a
participant.

In the baseline, all 26 roles support activation throughout. Under autonomous
source pressure, the coalition moves to 7 conditional positions and 19
deferrals, so the decision gate fails. With the same source process plus a
verified package resolving the four classes of requirements, all 26 return to
support.

The claim is deliberately narrow: this is a synthetic mechanism demonstration,
not evidence about human institutions. The interesting part is that the full
trajectory, source choices, agent rationales, and decision gate are inspectable.

Research case:
<https://brian-mac-mini.tail9c321e.ts.net/waltzman/?view=case>

My main question is whether this is a faithful minimal operationalization of
your shift from influence on beliefs to influence on the decision environment.
If you have 20 minutes, I would be glad to walk through it and hear what you
would change in the next experiment.

Best,

Brian
