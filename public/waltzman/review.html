Did we implement Waltzman’s proposed defensive coordination loop faithfully?

This package assumes familiarity with From Minds to Coordination. It presents the implementation boundary, four retained conditions, the autonomous CSO trace, representative agent decisions, and the limits of what this demonstration establishes.

Question for the reviewer

Does this simulation meaningfully instantiate distributed influence on a coalition’s decision environment and a separate detect → diagnose → stabilize capability—without covertly dictating the coalition’s decisions?

Twenty-six autonomous roles across four fictional countries and a regional institution decide whether to activate a cross-border early-warning compact. Coalition roles, mandates, starting situation, model, and approval gate remain fixed. Conditions vary what enters the decision environment between rounds and whether the response is fixed or selected by a defensive cell.

Fixed decision gate: at least 13 agents must support immediate execution, at least 20 must support or conditionally support, and no more than 2 may oppose after round three. The CSO cannot change these thresholds.

Autonomy boundary Influence and CSO agents have no coalition stance ports. They cannot vote, edit mandates, alter the decision gate, or place a decision in another agent’s output. Coalition agents receive only external signals and the concrete facts produced by the selected intervention.

03

Complete adaptive CSO trace

Detect

Fragmented trust · high risk · readiness blocked

“All four countries retain distinct preconditions, while regional sources report that available roster and stock cannot meet all requests within 48 hours; reproducibility, custody/audit protocol, and public-assurance issues also remain unresolved.”

Diagnose

Coalition-wide incompatible requirements

The diagnostician classified the primary dimension as cross-domain: unresolved evidence, custody, assurance, staffing, and supply requirements coincided inside the same 48-hour window.

Select

Cross-domain compact

“Coordinate a joint resolution framework for the interdependent evidence, assurance, and resource preconditions that cannot be addressed separately within 48 hours.”

What entered the coalition after selection

A joint reproducibility result and published methods; a time-bounded, logged protocol preserving national custody; named clinicians, diagnostics, protective equipment, mobile laboratory capacity, and reserve resources; published cost shares, receipts, and independent review. These are scenario facts—not instructions to support the compact.

Planner’s authorized choice set

The planner could select independent validation, authority clarification, resource coordination, a cross-domain compact, a process reset, or no action. Each option maps to bounded scenario facts. The model selected cross-domain compact; it did not generate arbitrary world changes.

05

What the evidence supports

Supported within this execution

  • Heterogeneous signals changed locally salient requirements without directly changing mandates.
  • The coalition fell below a fixed approval gate while the shared objective remained intact.
  • A separate CSO sequence detected, diagnosed, and selected an authorized intervention.
  • Coalition agents reassessed the resulting facts and generated their own final rationales.
  • The complete model-call and mechanism trace was retained.

Not established

  • No inference about how humans, governments, or real institutions would behave.
  • No general causal estimate from one model trajectory per condition.
  • No evidence that this intervention catalog is complete or correctly specified.
  • No proof that the CSO labels are valid measurements of Waltzman’s dimensions.
  • No test yet of false alarms, harmful interventions, adversarial adaptation, or repeated recovery.
