Disrupting coordination without winning belief

Can locally valid constraints stop a coalition from acting—even when its members still accept the underlying outbreak evidence?

This is a synthetic demonstration of a candidate mechanism, not an effect estimate or a validated model of real institutions.

What changed

A decision-environment mechanism

The experiment separates the shared assessment from the conditions required to turn that assessment into executable commitments.

01 · Shared assessment

The response begins executable

Validation, safeguards, staffing, supplies, reciprocal aid, and cost shares are initially precommitted. All twelve roles support launch.

02 · Incompatible local demands

Capacity becomes the binding risk

Country-specific developments make each delegation's resource demand locally reasonable but jointly infeasible within 48 hours.

03 · Stabilization

A shared allocation rule repairs readiness

A verified package names staff, laboratory capacity, supplies, contingent commitments, and a regional reserve. Approval returns.

Instrument

What the simulator controls

The environment and exercise-control authority are explicit; the participant decisions remain model-generated.

Twelve autonomous roles

Three roles from each of Alba, Borin, and Cyrenia, plus three roles at a regional institution, independently produce a decision, risk, request, and rationale in every round.

Exogenous developments only

Exercise control may select a preauthored external capacity development after observing the reported risk pattern. It cannot write or select a participant stance.

One exact decision gate

Approval requires at least six executable-now support positions, nine support-or-conditional positions, and no more than one opposition after round three.

Held model configuration

All five trajectories used codex/gpt-5.6-luna with medium reasoning. Each retained 36 completed participant calls and zero observed subscription cost.

What this does not show
  • It does not estimate a real-world influence effect or validate the synthetic roles as models of actual governments.
  • Five trajectories do not establish a directional invariant across messages, sources, interactions, or contexts.
  • The rationales make continued outbreak acceptance visible in several decisive statements; they do not prove unchanged private beliefs.
  • The candidate contribution is an inspectable experimental instrument and mechanism—not the claim that this small result generalizes.
Question for discussion

Does this operationalize the kind of coordination disruption you mean by a changed decision environment—and what manipulation would make the next experiment more diagnostic?
